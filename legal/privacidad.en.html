2. What data we process

  • Booking form: name, email, style, colour preference, size, body area, time slot and the message you write.
  • WhatsApp, phone or email: the information you give us when you contact us (for example, your number and what you tell us).
  • Technical data: when you visit the website, the hosting server logs basic technical data (such as your IP address) for security purposes.

Please do not include health information or other sensitive data in the form; if it is needed for your tattoo, we will discuss it in person at the studio.

3. Why we use it and legal basis

  • To answer your request and manage your appointment (quote, date, deposit). Legal basis: your consent when submitting the form and taking pre-contractual steps at your request.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as the informed consent required by Canary Islands health regulations (Decree 154/2004) before tattooing. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep the website secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

We do not use your data to send you advertising and we do not create profiles.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as needed to answer your request and manage your appointment. We then delete it, except for data we must keep to comply with legal obligations (for example, tax or health obligations), for the periods required by law.

5. Who else is involved

We do not sell or share your data with third parties unless required by law. To run the website we use these providers, which process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms: sends the form data to our email.
  • Google (Gmail): the email account where we receive and answer requests.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): website hosting.
  • Google Fonts: the website's typefaces; when they load, your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States). In that case, the transfer relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to abora.tattoo.art [at] gmail.com or at the studio. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Minors

Children under 14 may not give us their personal data without the consent of their parents or guardians. To tattoo a minor, a parent or legal guardian must come to the studio to sign the consent form.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising tools that track visitors, which is why you won't see a cookie banner. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your permission before using them.

9. Security and changes

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data. We may update this policy; the date of the latest update is shown above.
